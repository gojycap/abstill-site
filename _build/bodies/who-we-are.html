Who we are

The firm presently comprises Abhishek Singh, an atypical lawyer of twenty-two years.

His career spans private practice and in-house roles across the UAE, Southeast Asia and Africa.

His depth is at the intersection of financial services and stablecoins, TMT, arbitration, and corporate and M&A.

Abhishek is admitted in Singapore and Australia (NSW).

Some of the fun he has had — that can be disclosed

  • Design, regulatory and implementation strategy for a stablecoin-based neobank.
  • M&A and strategic transactions in Egypt, Ghana, Tanzania and Rwanda for Airtel Africa.
  • Stablecoin-based GP–LP fund structures with tax-optimised financing of LP returns.
  • A tax-optimised joint-venture structure for a consortium in Singapore's fibre-to-the-home rollout.
  • Persuading the regulator to modify investor-disclosure rules in a rare hostile takeover attempt in Singapore's telecom sector.
